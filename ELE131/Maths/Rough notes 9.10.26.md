
A pair of linear simultaneous equations takes the form:
- $a_1x+b_1y=c_1$
- $a_2x+b_2y=c_2$

Plotting these as lines means:
- Intersection = 2 solutions
- Parallel = no solutions
- Same line = infinite number of solutions

Solve by substitution or elimination.

Simultaneous equations are inconsistent if there is no solution.
E.g:
- $3x+2y=4$
- $3x+2y=6$

ill-conditioning




Use matrix methods to more easily solve simultaneous equations with many unknown (i.e. more than 2).
