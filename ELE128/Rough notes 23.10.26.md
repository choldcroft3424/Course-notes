Use superposition analysis when you have multiple current and/or voltage sources. In superposition analysis, we consider one source at a time. We replace other voltage sources with a short circuit (i.e. a wire) and other current sources with an open circuit (i.e. a gap)

Consider each voltage and current source one at a time to find unknowns (current and voltage). Then add all voltages and currents together to get the overall voltage and current.

Note you can't find power with this method.
